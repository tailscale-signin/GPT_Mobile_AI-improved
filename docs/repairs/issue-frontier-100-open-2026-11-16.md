### Disposition and closure rules

1. **Fixed:** close as completed only after corresponding changed-head tests pass and the repaired behavior is observed on an updated build or appropriate external system; link proof in the issue.
2. **Diagnostic-limited:** retain the exact missing evidence and a safe recapture procedure; once enhanced reporting is deployed, close as `not_planned` only with a documented diagnostic disposition and a clear reopen trigger. Never relabel a transient service failure as fixed by code.
3. **Duplicates:** merge issue evidence into a canonical cause and close only when the duplicate relationship is supported by typed stage/code fingerprints rather than title alone.
4. **Merge:** draft PR stays open until all required checks, security/reviews, task-specific behavior evidence and exact-head readiness are verified. A green historical SHA or model claim is not sufficient.
