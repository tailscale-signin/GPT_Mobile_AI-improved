MediaPipe speech models
======================

The application downloads these optional models only after an explicit install action.
Model files are not included in the APK. Immutable source revisions, expected sizes and
SHA-256 hashes are recorded in MediaPipeSpeechCatalog.kt.

Kokoro-82M
Original model: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Conversion: litert-community/Kokoro-82M
https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Kokoro-82M/tree/bf1c2405642aa310bd210205c22d3ae1abc253fb
License: Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
The LiteRT community converted and quantized the original weights and packaged the
frontends and voice assets. This app uses the CPU/GPU-compatible bundle unchanged.

Phonon 31M English
Original model: Gradium, https://huggingface.co/gradium/phonon-31m-en
Conversion: litert-community/phonon-31m-en
https://huggingface.co/litert-community/phonon-31m-en/tree/ee374c33f0f6f8ed1d9f761e21cdb97df6accfe8
License: Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
The LiteRT community converted and quantized the original model and packaged its
tokenizer, codec and voice conditioning. This app uses the converted bundle unchanged.

Pocket TTS English
Original project and voice assets: Kyutai, https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts
and https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices
Conversion: litert-community/pocket-tts
https://huggingface.co/litert-community/pocket-tts/tree/00c866f2397788f514ab6bbcb931be22afc3b58f
Model license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
The LiteRT community converted and quantized the model, codec, tokenizer and voice
conditioning into a single English bundle. This app uses that bundle unchanged.
Attribution does not imply endorsement by Kyutai or the conversion authors.
