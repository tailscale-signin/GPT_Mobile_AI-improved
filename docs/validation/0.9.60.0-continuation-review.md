### Distinct recovered benchmark-rendering crash

The original diagnostic JSONL records a fatal result-rendering incident at **2026-10-24T19:37:18.347Z**: tag `AppCrash`, category `fatal`, severity `critical`, main thread, `java.util.UnknownFormatConversionException`. Correlated Android entries report `Conversion = 'End of String'` and the stack `String.format(String.java:4714)` → `BenchmarkResultCardKt.BenchmarkResultCard$lambda$4(BenchmarkResultCard.kt:36)`. Two retained copies have the same event identity and timestamp; they represent one recorded incident. This is direct evidence of a benchmark-card formatting failure, with no correlated benchmark-export action or export-specific stack.

The existing integrated repair routes `BenchmarkResultCard` metrics through `benchmarkMetricText`: only fixed numeric patterns reach `String.format`, while `%`, placeholder-like labels and other units are appended as literal text. Fresh `continuation-full-unit-v2` XML confirms **2/2 `BenchmarkMetricFormatTest` cases and 5/5 `BenchmarkResultCardTest` cases passed**, with no failures, errors or skips in either class. The cases cover literal percent/placeholder units, US/German locales, absent/nonfinite measurements and the actual collapsed/expanded percentage card. These specific passes do not imply that the full suite passed or that the phone was retested.

The recovered file contains no OOM, `JsonToStringWriter`, `CursorWindow` or `TransactionTooLarge` signature, including its rejoined continuation records. The trigger and cause of the separately reported benchmark-export crash remain unestablished. The synthetic export-allocation reproduction and streaming/export regressions are separate evidence from this actual rendering incident.
