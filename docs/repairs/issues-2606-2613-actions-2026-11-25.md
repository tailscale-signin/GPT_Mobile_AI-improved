The actual production serializer reproduced the public report's loss of all
four blocker totals when deadline, preparation, publication and failed-event
fields competed for the 3,072-byte envelope. A synthetic 70-criterion fixture
produced a 3,069-byte report with no retained blocker totals before the fix.

sample with truthful omission counts. The fixture's distribution is synthetic;
the redacted reports do not disclose the missing real distributions.

The regression also checks the JSON roundtrip, private-identifier exclusion and
that absent totals are not invented. The change adds no public fields, enlarges
no envelope, grants no time or mutation authority and changes no acceptance
verdict.
