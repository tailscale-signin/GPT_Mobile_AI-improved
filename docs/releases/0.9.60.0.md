## Provider privacy and personal-data transfer

Provider activation uses typed retention policies and device-local acknowledgement receipts, including compatible/custom profiles that resolve to known free routes. Activation is separate from permission to read or transfer personal data. A grant binds the selected profile, effective model, configured endpoint, credential identity, recipient, categories, chat/profile scope and expiry. Health grants also bind a permitted time range; coarse and precise location remain distinct. Legacy boolean preferences cannot authorize a new scoped profile grant.

Permission is checked before a personal tool executes and at final dispatch. Provenance survives retained tool results, timeline checkpoints, recovery pages and delegation; derived queries and summaries retain the sensitivity of their inputs. Location-based downstream map lookup requires its own named downstream authorization. Public research remains subject to the current chat, plugin and public-web switches.

Consent receipts are excluded from portable backup authority and invalidated on settings restore. Startup reconciliation disables unacknowledged or retired executable routes while retaining history, profile identity and explicit OFF choices. PorchLM/OpenCode catalog entries and exact retired endpoint aliases remain blocked.

Entry points include `ProviderPrivacyPolicy`, `FreeModelToolConsentStore`, `PersonalDataGuardedTool`, `PersonalDataProvenance`, `AgentToolResolver` and `ConfiguredDispatchAuthority`. The [design ledger](../validation/0.9.60.0-design-coverage.md) names their regressions and transport integration.

## Provider limits, accepted jobs and Research recovery

Request/token reservations, usage settlement, rolling windows, provider cooldowns and credential/model health are persisted with their scopes. Retry-After seconds and dates retain deadline evidence; the UI shows current eligibility and dispatch rechecks current authority.

Provider waits cover never-dispatched work and a typed Research stage rejected with **known non-acceptance**. Research checkpoints preserve the exact stage/work item, frozen prompt, source version, packet cursor, accepted claims and spent budget. A resumed run skips completed acquisition and packets, then revalidates current permissions and ownership before continuing the unfinished operation. Ambiguous transmission is not converted into permission to send a duplicate request.

Saved waits retain their original authorization through the asynchronous launch, preparation, physical dispatch, another quota wait and completion. A permission change interrupts that saved work for review. Small wall/monotonic clock differences no longer prevent a valid never-started request from entering its durable wait; scheduling projections remain separate from immutable provider evidence.

An accepted queued job keeps its remote job identity across restart and permission-revision changes. Polling revalidates current authority, retains the original accepted deadline and records submission, polling and cleanup separately. Known network recovery closes the active DNS episode without erasing earlier failures. Cooldown waits do not require a foreground service to remain alive.

Research recovery uses immutable, hashed evidence corpora and bounded page reads. Stage statistics distinguish logical work, model attempts, actual physical dispatch, queue submissions/polls, cached results and usage provenance. Historical observations without trustworthy request identity remain unknown.

## Combined answers and source inspection

Each Combined turn has a canonical final-message identity, while original contributor attempts and historical revisions remain available. Strict single-pass final generation remains the default. Append continuation is a separate, explicitly accepted versioned policy with a durable writer cursor and segment progress; it does not silently replace the writer, replay acquisition tools or change a saved policy.

The final answer, inspector and attribution colours share the claim-to-answer mapping. Source accounting remains separate from factual verification. Matching preserves event/date relationships, quantities, negation, qualifiers, conflicting assertions, Unicode, code and tables. A cleaned editorial view removes exact repetition and identifiable drafting residue while retaining original source ranges and raw replies. Complementary contributor instructions retain the original task. Grouped collection decisions allow retained usable replies to proceed and permit exclusion for the current turn.

Compressed named events with parenthetical dates receive bounded partial attribution, so a short mention does not claim complete coverage of its source. Saved completed answers with unmet requirements offer an explicit revised answer instead of an unfinished-record resume. This decision uses the selected revision's matching report and digest, preserving historical receipts and compact document references.

Large finals and original contributions are stored as verified document pages. Final rendering, shared-source inspection and contributor-tab switching read bounded ranges. A contributor tab no longer resolves the full final message or all contributors to show one source. Explicit document copy/export still obtains complete text; source copy reads and verifies the selected original only. Shared-contribution controls identify every participating profile and expose accessible labels.

`CombinedOperationTrace` records structural operation ownership and timing for staging, block reads, source extraction/mapping, conflict scans, protocol decode, source-tab transition/loading and Markdown measurement/layout. Perfetto slices and opt-in diagnostic records contain identifiers and hashes, not source bodies. Frequent diagnostic timings are sampled by a duration threshold; those logs are not a complete latency distribution.

Prompt admission accounts for the final rendered input, tools/history, safety reserve and output allowance. Typed deadline reasons retain native progress and cleanup evidence. Backend-aware process supervision continues after the first visible delta; native, IPC and client bounds retain their distinct clocks and scopes. Results expose planned/accounted/executed coverage, failure-only attempts, actual runtime receipts, metric populations and the separate last verified result. ## Compatibility, persistence and backups

Room schema **47** uses the additive **46→47** migration. Published schema 46 remains unchanged. New storage includes durable blocks, delivery records, final manifests, a final block index and compact manifest references on messages/checkpoints.

Payload storage version 2 reuses content-addressed pages within the owning run while retaining an immutable logical order, length and digest for each revision. Version 1 payloads remain readable. Source references carry their own payload descriptor and deduplicated Markdown frame references, so reopening a page can restore code-fence/table context without hydrating the source body. Page limits are storage/rendering boundaries, not a cap on the retained document.

Conversation-only backups retain referenced current and archived manifests, source/frame payloads, nested timeline payloads and the minimal owning run rows. Restore validates ownership, ordering and hashes before replacing destination data. Portable JSON export materializes complete referenced text and removes storage references that would otherwise be orphaned on another installation. Research recovery corpus inclusion follows the applicable history/research backup selections. Device-local consent and credentials are not embedded in those corpus exports.
