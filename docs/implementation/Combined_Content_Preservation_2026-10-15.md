# Combined content preservation and normal-mode source indicators

Implements the supplied Great Emu War findings on `fix/feature-audit-completion-2026-10-15`, draft PR #690. The private export is not committed. Its five model-generated historical replies and selected answer are reduced to an anonymous behavioral fixture: `app/src/test/resources/combined/great-emu-war.json`. Account/profile UUIDs, provider configuration, request traces and private metadata are excluded. Historical assertions in that fixture are not treated as factual evidence.

## Behavior

* **Comprehensive is the default for new synthesis inputs.** The Advanced Settings toggle “Comprehensive Combined Answers” can restore request-length priority. The policy is written into the immutable synthesis envelope, so changing the setting cannot change a previously frozen retry. Legacy envelopes keep request-length behavior. The comprehensive priority also survives the finite-context multipass handoff. The prompt explicitly preserves different relevant examples, groups overlapping subjects, integrates complementary details and relaxes a short word goal when it would remove entire topics.
* **Completion requires omission checks.** `CombinedContentCoverage` inventories all substantive source sentences, combines exact duplicate occurrences while retaining every submitting profile, and checks their textual representation within answer paragraphs. Numeric tokens and qualification words must survive approximate textual matches. Work exceeding the comparison allowance fails closed rather than dropping inventory. The actual one-topic answer fails against the five original inputs; a four-topic answer retaining both Meiji accounts passes.
* **Repair is bounded.** `CombinedDeliverySession` buffers editorial output, forwards usage/progress, and allows one tool-free repair by the pinned primary under the existing context, authorization, output and invocation budgets. Repair has a 90-second deadline. A second incomplete draft cannot emit `Completed`: the original draft is retained with `COMBINED_COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE`, and the original contributions remain accessible. Permission is rechecked before releasing retained content. Committed prefixes cannot be replaced. Ordinary conversations keep streaming.
* **Frozen answers cannot be silently replaced.** The UI reservation no longer admits changed sources while an admitted synthesis owns the turn. Existing terminal synthesis inputs never automatically resynthesize after helper changes. The persistence transaction additionally rejects a new corpus without explicit replacement/recovery authority, and initial freezing rechecks every admitted contributor against its completed durable run and message text in that transaction. Settling source writes retry before dispatch, rather than replacing a completed answer.
* **Profile identity is always visible.** Source bubbles, source prose and the contribution inspector use the persistent numbered profile palette outside Debug Mode. Status dots retain their separate completion/error meaning.
* **Passage badges are honest about their basis.** Completed final paragraphs can show clickable, consistently coloured badges for every profile containing the identical sentence. Shared wording keeps all matching submitters. Projection identity binds badges to the current passage and full frozen sources, preventing stale badges during asynchronous recomputation. Paraphrases remain neutral; badges are explicitly labelled text matches. Original answer text, copying and export are unchanged. The inspector remains available for unknown or unavailable semantic support.

**These checks are textual omission detection, not full claim entailment.** They may conservatively reject valid paraphrases, and passing them cannot prove factual correctness, scientific evidence quality or exclusive ownership of meaning. `semanticCoverageVerified=false` remains explicit. Qualified semantic attribution, full multimodal coverage, device rendering/accessibility qualification and live five-provider reproduction remain open. No verification qualification or release gate is fabricated or relaxed.
